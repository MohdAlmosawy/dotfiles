# UOB LaTeX reference

Source for the page: دليل كتابة الأطروحات العلمية في جامعة البحرين, عمادة الدراسات العليا, 2014.

Source for citations: APA 7th edition (Publication Manual; Purdue OWL APA guide). A Zotero paper's `references.bib` is generated by Better BibTeX as Better BibLaTeX. These patterns describe that export. Do not hand-edit an auto-exported file.

## Bibliography entries

DOI field is the identifier only. biblatex-apa prints `https://doi.org/...`.

```bibtex
@article{author2020,
  author  = {Author, A. A. and Author, B. B.},
  title   = {Title of the article in sentence case},
  journal = {Journal Title in Title Case},
  year    = {2020},
  volume  = {12},
  number  = {3},
  pages   = {1--10},
  doi     = {10.0000/example}
}

@inproceedings{author2026paper,
  author    = {Author, A. A.},
  title     = {Title of the paper in sentence case},
  booktitle = {Proceedings of the Conference Name},
  year      = {2026},
  publisher = {Publisher Name},
  doi       = {10.0000/example}
}

@book{author2019,
  author    = {Author, A. A.},
  title     = {Title of the book in sentence case},
  year      = {2019},
  publisher = {Publisher Name},
  edition   = {2}
}

@online{author2024web,
  author       = {Author, A. A.},
  title        = {Title of the page in sentence case},
  year         = {2024},
  organization = {Website Name},
  url          = {https://example.org/page}
}
```

No date: `year = {n.d.}`. Up to 20 authors are listed. Do not add "Retrieved from" unless the source is unarchived and expected to change.

## Tables and figures

APA 7 captions on a UOB course paper:

- Table number bold, italic title, both above the table. Note below.
- Figure number and italic title above the figure. Note below.
- Number tables and figures separately.
- Place each float after the paragraph that first mentions it. Do not collect them at the end.

## Thesis order

Use this only when the user is writing the thesis, not a course paper.

1. Arabic title page
2. Approval page
3. Arabic abstract, at most one page, 12 pt
4. Dedication
5. Acknowledgement
6. Contents
7. List of tables
8. List of figures, if any
9. Body
10. Conclusion and recommendations
11. References
12. Appendices
13. English abstract, at most one page, 12 pt
14. English title page at the back

Page numbers sit at the bottom centre. Preliminary pages use letters. Numbering of the body starts at 1 after those pages. A chapter-opening page is counted and the number is not printed. Numbering stops at the end of the references; appendices are not numbered.

The 2014 guide limits a theoretical thesis to about 80,000 words and an applied thesis to about 40,000 words.

Every quotation names its source and the page. Alphabetical order in the reference list ignores the Arabic article ال when sorting.
